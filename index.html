Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Kashmiri silk rugs special?

Kashmiri silk rugs are knotted by hand in pure silk, one knot at a time. Fine knotting gives sharp detail, and natural silk has a soft sheen that changes with the light. Many “silk” rugs sold online are machine-made from viscose. These look similar at first but wear and fade quickly.

How much does a hand-knotted silk rug cost?

Our silk rugs start from [₹ X]. The price depends on size, knot count and design, because these decide how long a rug takes to make. A large, finely knotted rug can spend many months on the loom. Send us an enquiry for an exact price, including delivery to your country.

How can I tell if a silk rug is real?

Turn the rug over. A hand-knotted rug shows small, slightly uneven knots on the back, and the fringe is part of the rug, not sewn on. Real silk feels cool and smooth. Viscose, often sold as “art silk”, feels slippery and loses its shine. Every Kashmir Imperial rug comes with a certificate.

Yes. We make bespoke silk rugs to your exact size, colours and design. You approve the design drawing first, then we send photo and video updates while it is on the loom. Most bespoke rugs take [X–Y] months, depending on size and knot count.

Is your Pashmina genuine?

Yes. Our Pashmina is hand-spun and hand-woven in Kashmir from the fine undercoat of Changthangi goats in Ladakh. It carries the GI tag for Kashmir Pashmina. We never sell shahtoosh, which is illegal because it comes from an endangered antelope.
